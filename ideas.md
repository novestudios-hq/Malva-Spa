# Malva Spa — Design Direction

## Chosen approach: Hacienda Herbarium

### Design Movement

**Hacienda Herbarium** combines the patina and tactile restraint of Mexican modernism with editorial botanical illustration. Its visual story is a private treatment room set within a well-loved coastal hacienda, rendered in dark forest green, sand, oxidized brass, and earthenware hues.

### Core Principles

The experience is **quietly luxurious rather than ostentatious**, using shadow, surface, and generous negative space to create atmosphere. It will use **editorial asymmetry**, avoiding repetitive centered blocks in favor of offset content frames and tall vertical moments. Every interface detail should feel **tactile and intentional**, from thin botanical linework to softly weathered card surfaces. Finally, the site will preserve **clarity over ornament**, so menus and pricing remain easy to scan.

### Color Philosophy

The near-black forest green #3B4232 establishes a sheltered, restorative mood and gives warm type an elegant glow. Sage #818B6F creates softened tonal shifts across surfaces, while sand #F5F2EB provides a natural light source for readable copy and paper-like menu cards. Terracotta #8B4513 is reserved for prices, small accents, and moments of invitation, echoing fired clay and human warmth.

### Layout Paradigm

The page is conceived as a **slow vertical garden walk**. A cinematic, asymmetric hero sets the atmosphere; menus unfold as a layered sequence of paper folios, narrow service index rails, and varied editorial columns. Wide screens use intentional offsets and visual pauses; mobile collapses into an elegant single-column reading rhythm without losing hierarchy.

### Signature Elements

The system repeats three visual motifs: **a discreet botanical seal** made from a malva flower and leaf, **fine herbarium-style linework** that quietly drifts at section edges, and **terracotta price markers** that punctuate each treatment like small found objects.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions are calm and precise. Navigation links reveal a fine sand-colored line; cards lift almost imperceptibly while gaining a warmer edge; the mobile menu opens as a dark, composed panel rather than a generic dropdown. Calls to action have firm, tactile pressed states.

### Animation

Reveal motion is understated and unhurried: sections rise 12px with opacity transitions, staggered between 40–70ms. Leaf elements may drift subtly only where motion is permitted. Hover and press transitions stay under 220ms using a confident ease-out curve. All nonessential movement is disabled for reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System

**Cormorant Garamond** brings a high-contrast, editorial presence to section headers and treatment names. **Manrope** supports clear modern body copy, navigation, durations, and prices. The **Allura** script face is used only for the Malva Spa wordmark, never for functional text. Headers use spacious tracking and expressive scale; prices use compact uppercase numerals with a slightly increased letter spacing.

### Brand Essence

**Malva Spa is Puerto Vallarta’s botanical hideaway for restorative treatments, meticulous nail care, and unhurried shared rituals.**

Personality adjectives: **earthy, intimate, assured**.

### Brand Voice

The voice is inviting, serene, and specific, never sales-heavy. Headlines should be poetic but restrained; CTAs should feel like a graceful invitation rather than an instruction.

> “Una pausa para volver a ti.”

> “Reserva un ritual a tu ritmo.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The mark is a single **malva bloom inside a low, open arch**, drawn with a thin organic line and a tiny offset leaf. The accompanying wordmark uses a loose, elegant handwritten flourish, with “Spa” balanced in small modern sans-serif capitals beneath it. The identity is immediately recognizable even when used as the small header mark.

### Signature Brand Color

**Malva Forest — #3B4232**. A deep, softened olive that conveys shade, foliage, and privacy, and anchors the entire brand system.
